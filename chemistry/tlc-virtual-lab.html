Meet thin-layer chromatography

A TLC plate is a sheet coated with a thin layer of silica, which is very polar. This is the stationary phase. A small spot of each sample goes on a pencil line near the bottom (the origin), and the plate stands in a shallow pool of solvent, the mobile phase. The solvent soaks up the plate and carries each compound with it. Compounds that are strongly attracted to the polar silica move slowly; compounds that dissolve well in the mobile phase move further.
