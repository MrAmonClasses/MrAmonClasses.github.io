Part A

Order of operations: × and ÷ first

Slide 3

Worked example

Calculate 18 + 6 × 4 − 10 ÷ 2

  1. × and ÷ first: 6 × 4 = 24 and 10 ÷ 2 = 5
  2. Rewrite: 18 + 24 − 5
  3. Then + and − from left to right: 18 + 24 = 42, 42 − 5 = 37

18 + 6 × 4 − 10 ÷ 2 = 37

Tip: × and ÷ come before + and −. When there are only × and ÷ (or only + and −), work left to right.

Part B

Order of operations: brackets first

Slide 4

Worked example

Calculate (15 − 7) × 4 + 6

  1. Brackets first: 15 − 7 = 8
  2. Then ×: 8 × 4 = 32
  3. Then +: 32 + 6 = 38

(15 − 7) × 4 + 6 = 38

Tip: Order: Brackets, then × and ÷ (left to right), then + and − (left to right).

Part C

Using the problem-solving cycle

Slide 5

Worked example

A floor is 3 m × 2.4 m. Tiles are 30 cm × 30 cm, sold in boxes of 10 for $42. Add 10% extra for cuts and breakages. What will the tiles cost?

  1. Identify: find how many tiles fit, add 10%, round up to whole boxes, then find the cost. Change metres to cm: 300 cm × 240 cm.
  2. Act: 300 ÷ 30 = 10 tiles across, 240 ÷ 30 = 8 tiles down, 10 × 8 = 80 tiles
  3. 10% extra: 80 + 8 = 88 tiles. 8 boxes only holds 80, so buy 9 boxes (90 tiles).
  4. Cost: 9 × $42 = $378
  5. Evaluate: estimate 9 boxes × $40 = $360. $378 is close, so it is reasonable.

Communicate: Buy 9 boxes of tiles for $378.

Tip: Use all four stages: Identify, Act, Evaluate, Communicate. Finish with a sentence.

Part D

Pay with overtime

Slide 6

Worked example

Sam works 38 hours at $24.50 an hour, plus 4 hours overtime at time and a half. What is Sam’s pay?

  1. Normal pay: 38 × $24.50 = $931.00
  2. Overtime rate: $24.50 × 1.5 = $36.75 an hour
  3. Overtime pay: 4 × $36.75 = $147.00
  4. Total: $931.00 + $147.00 = $1078.00

Sam’s pay is $1078.00

Tip: Time and a half = × 1.5. Double time = × 2. Find the overtime rate first.
